\begin{abstract}
Fine-grained surgical skill assessment can provide targeted feedback, but
manual evaluation is time-consuming. Existing approaches to automating such
assessment often predict scores directly without explicitly deriving
quantitative measures linked to assessment criteria. We propose an
event-guided method that estimates three needle-hold measures from monocular
suturing videos and maps them to End-to-End Assessment of Suturing Expertise
(EASE) scores. A general-purpose vision--language model (VLM), prompted with a
few examples, detects events and provides keypoints and needle masks without
task-specific training. Within event-defined assessment windows, geometric
analysis estimates Needle Hold Ratio, Needle Hold Angle, and Depth of Needle
Hold, with each score traceable to its source frames and measurements. On
surgeon-disjoint JIGSAWS test data, fully automated scoring achieved AUCs of
0.89--0.97 against physician ratings, matching or exceeding a state-of-the-art
VLM given annotated windows and scored examples.
\end{abstract}
