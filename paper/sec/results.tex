\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:main} compares our method with direct VLM grading on the
annotated assessment windows. Despite the favorable conditions given to the
baseline, our method has a higher AUC and balanced accuracy for all three
sub-skills. The difference is clearest for Needle Hold Angle (AUC $+0.119$,
95\% CI 0.05--0.23), where the baseline misses many poor holds (recall of
EASE 1: 0.58 versus 0.83); for the other two, the intervals are not clearly
above zero, so our method is at least on par. Measuring from the 5-fps frames
only, as the baseline sees them, changes our test AUCs by less than 0.04.

Table~\ref{tab:e2e} shows the end-to-end results, in which the events are also
detected. Event detection reaches an F1 of 0.89, and every annotated stitch is
paired with a detected stitch (median entry error 0.28~s), with one extra
detection. The AUCs differ by at most 0.012 from those with the annotated
windows, and all four sub-skills are scored from the video alone.
